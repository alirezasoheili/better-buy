Research continued on 10 October 2026, **without admitting any campaign**. The previously rejected Papia campaign was neither requested again nor enabled. The threshold remains **40%**, the server-owned default remains Market Party, and the UI is unchanged. Account-plan verification was not attempted in this investigation and does not determine the feed research results.

It needs only four nominal concrete requests including terminal, versus nine for the dairy candidate's two qualifying offers. However, replay exposed duplicated pagination identities, conflicting nonqualifying vendor metadata and unresolved benefit applicability. ## Method and scope

Each run obtained access through the existing `collectDeals` guest flow, collected the baseline, then discovered current home collections for the same saved test coordinates. Tokens, device identifiers, coordinates and customer identifiers were never included in the saved research evidence. IDs were resolved from each live home response; the dated IDs below are observations, not production configuration.

Read-only calls were sequential, with no fan-out, no redirect following, no account API calls and no production/local persistence of offers. Requests used the allowlisted origin `https://svc.snapp.market`. A 401, 403 or 429 would stop probing. Concrete/discovery responses had a 4 MiB response guard; each run had a 40-request/45-second research ceiling. No run approached those ceilings.

The first three comparison runs began at **12:55:09.431Z, 12:56:32.431Z and 12:58:17.022Z**. Each baseline returned **83 eligible offers, 350 observed products and 35 vendors**, completing in 410, 401 and 432 ms respectively. These are paired same-location windows, not a union of observations taken at unrelated times. Thirteen distinct campaigns were examined. Larger feeds were sampled at first/second/boundary/terminal pages; promising bounded feeds were followed through every nominal page.

Home discovery used `/express-home/mobile/v3/user/new-home`. Concrete collection used `/express-search/mobile/v1/product/product-list`, guest/PWA parameters, `lat`, `long`, `page`, `page_size=100`, and the endpoint-specific JSON `extra-filter` documented in the spec: dynamically discovered `product_list_id`, category/brand/chain/vendor IDs zero, and `new_home=true`. The observed envelope was `status=true`, `data.count`, and `data.finalResult` containing uppercase `PRODUCT` rows. Effective size was ten, despite requesting 100; shorter first pages contained the whole small count. None of these responses establishes the semantics of another adapter.

## Identity conflict investigation and applicability

In the initial Rani snapshot, three 38% rows had outer vendor **117199** but nested vendor **115641**; another 20% row had outer **114624** but nested **109771**. Their `document_id` repeated the outer product/vendor pair and `sub_vendor_id` was null. These repetitions do not independently prove stock/price ownership. None of these rows was among the nine qualifying candidates.

A replay at **13:02:11.142Z** changed the below-threshold conflicts and returned **27 rows but only 26 distinct pairs**, including repeated `115356:5318892`. A follow-up at **13:02:46.509Z** again had 27 distinct rows and no mismatched nested vendors. The successful follow-up does not erase the inconsistent replay or justify a universal ID substitution.

Independent store-home calls established `p4zk4y→118114`, `0wqqvv→115600`, `0wqw55→114624`, and `32q8kd→109771`. The first two corroborate qualifying candidate vendor metadata. The last two confirm that the outer and nested IDs in a sampled conflict refer to **different real stores**, rather than interchangeable aliases. Store homes reported `doesDeliver=true` for those four requested-context stores. This evidence must not be generalized to every campaign row.

To seek exact product/stock/price ownership, the known store-campaign path `/express-search/product-list/{live_campaign_id}/vendor/{validated_code}` was tested with a five-row page. The initial request returned **422**, explicitly requiring query `lat` and `long`; that was a request-contract issue, not a forbidden response. Adding those exact required fields produced **204 without a usable body** at the first qualifying store. Its empty-response/filter contract is unverified, so this establishes neither absence nor terminal behavior and does not resolve ownership. No ambiguous vendor row was rewritten or integrated. Conflicting tuples remain quarantined pending a valid independent stock-backed confirmation contract.

All sampled campaigns lacked explicit product-level eligibility/validity/coupon terms. Dairy's qualifying rows carried a `BEST_PRICE` badge; Rani's qualifying badges were empty. Neither is affirmative applicability evidence. Store 115600 exposed `is_pro=true` and shipping-method Pro metadata, while other sampled stores exposed false; those describe store/shipping features and cannot establish whether a particular item discount requires Pro. Guest access returning a price likewise does not prove unrestricted purchase eligibility. No condition was inferred from a title or silently inherited from the baseline's legacy applicable-Market-Party default. No currency field was supplied; amounts are retained in the existing Snapp source units, with new contract unit validation still required.

For product namespace evidence, the dairy variants and several Pemina IDs also appeared with identical variant titles in baseline `productVariationId` rows. This supports a dated observed mapping for those variants, not an unrestricted alias rule for every campaign `id`. Rani's nine IDs have no baseline counterpart in this window, so their canonical product mapping still needs independent confirmation. [Identity audit and source references](better-buy-s05-identity-research.md).

## Decision and verification

Prioritize Rani's contract investigation next: establish stable count/order/terminal coverage; obtain valid store-scoped concrete confirmations for stock/price ownership and product IDs; verify item benefit applicability and units. Resolve vendor conflicts before admission. Once those feed gates pass, separately verify/enforce the combined executor/account budget and integrate one campaign into the frozen server-owned manifest and existing feed. No category/campaign UI is needed.

This continuation issued **103 sequential provider requests** across bounded comparison/corroboration runs, including one earlier nine-request attempt and the request-contract correction. There were **zero account-plan API requests**. The saved fixture excludes credentials and coordinates; the earlier aborted attempt's raw evidence was replaced, and its request count is disclosed rather than merged into coverage. There were no forbidden/rate-limit responses; store-offer 422/204 results remained unverified.

Added a research-only [offline analyzer](../apps/worker/scripts/snapp-campaign-research.mjs) and [six tests](../apps/worker/scripts/snapp-campaign-research.test.mjs), using the sanitized fixture. They verify nine apparent additions, the distinction between zero eligibility and all numerical duplicates, incomplete duplicate pagination, duplicate-only samples that cannot establish a whole-campaign result, vendor-conflict quarantine, missing stock, invalid money and the fixed threshold. This analyzer has no network calls and is not imported by the production collector, manifest or UI.

**45 relevant tests passed**: six offline research tests plus 39 authenticated/default-feed integration tests. Formatting and whitespace checks passed. File-hash validation confirms all pre-existing tracked/nonignored files are unchanged, including prior research, the UI and local implementation changes. No adapter was enabled or production code/configuration changed.
