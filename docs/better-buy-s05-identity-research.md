# S05 identity and applicability research

Observed 10 October 2026. This note separates read-only feed identity research from account-plan verification. It does not authorize campaign admission, lower the 40% threshold, or change the automatic feed/UI. New live campaign results are recorded by the parent investigation; this note owns the identity and applicability interpretation.

## What the current source proves

The existing Market Party adapter receives a vendor-grouped envelope, but saved offer identity comes from each **product**'s `vendorId` and `productVariationId`. The outer group's `vendor_id` controls page/vendor collection; it does not supply saved offer identity. The product also supplies `vendorCode`, `vendorTitle`, price, discount amount, ratio and stock. Missing stock defaults to zero in this established adapter. Its eligibility predicate is supplied ratio at least the threshold, positive stock, and a false out-of-stock flag. These facts describe existing behavior, not an independent contract for campaign responses. [Market Party model](../apps/worker/src/providers/snapp/model.ts), [eligibility predicate](../apps/worker/src/domain/eligibility.ts), [collector](../apps/worker/src/providers/snapp/collector.ts).

The saved v2 projection deduplicates by provider, vendor ID, product/variation ID, **benefit kind and benefit identity**. Product grouping separately includes the safely normalized variant title. A differing title signature is a diagnostic and split, not evidence of another interchangeable product. Price differences keep the latest successfully observed complete tuple, with deterministic tie breaking; stock must not be summed across repeated sightings. [v2 projection](../packages/shared/src/groupingV2.ts), [D05–D06](better-buy-expansion-spec.md#d05-retain-offer-conditions-and-provenance).

The projection supplies legacy Snapp rows with omitted benefit metadata the default `{kind: "market_party", identity: "ordinary", applicable: true, terms: null}`. That compatibility default is **not evidence that a concrete campaign has ordinary universally applicable Market Party terms**. A future campaign adapter must populate verified benefit metadata explicitly; otherwise an unknown campaign could receive a false applicability claim merely by flowing through existing code. Unknown/inapplicable benefits and conditional benefits are separated from the best ordinary headline offer by the projection. [v2 projection](../packages/shared/src/groupingV2.ts), [shared record contract](../packages/shared/src/contracts.ts).

## Counting rules for the new campaign comparison

Report the following separately for every discovered campaign and retain the same location and narrow observation window:

| Count                                   | Required evidence                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| --------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Stock-backed threshold rows             | Explicit positive numeric stock, explicit false out-of-stock flag, coherent finite nonnegative integer amounts, original amount greater than zero, and unchanged 40% threshold. A missing ratio can be derived from the amount tuple with its evidence recorded; it is not rounded up for eligibility. |
| Provisional unique threshold identities | Distinct campaign `(vendor_id, id)` pairs after reporting duplicates and conflicted IDs. This is a research namespace, not an approved saved identity.                                                                                                                                                 |
| Apparent baseline overlap               | Numerical product/store ID match to baseline product `vendorId/productVariationId`; report whether normalized variant titles, store codes and money tuples also agree. Without namespace/benefit proof this is provisional overlap.                                                                    |
| Confirmed eligible overlap              | Verified canonical product and vendor identities **and matching applicable benefit semantics**. Different price observations can still be the same offer if the benefit identity is the same.                                                                                                          |
| Apparent net-new threshold identities   | Provisional threshold identities absent from the numerical baseline identity set. Conflicted identities and unresolved benefit conditions must be disclosed; this is an upper bound on useful additions.                                                                                               |
| Confirmed net-new eligible offers       | Threshold, stock, money, canonical identity and applicability all verified, excluding confirmed same-benefit baseline offers.                                                                                                                                                                          |

These definitions implement the spec's G06 exact-identity comparison within matching benefit semantics. A title-only or product-only match is not an offer duplicate. Conversely, membership in two collection IDs is provenance, not automatically two benefits or twice the stock. An incomplete paged sample cannot establish "all duplicates," even if every observed row numerically matches: later pages may add offers. [D04–D06 and G06](better-buy-expansion-spec.md#d04-discount-and-stock-normalization).

For a complete snapshot, state **zero eligible offers** when no row passes the unchanged stock/threshold gate. State **eligible offers, all duplicates** only when at least one eligible offer exists and every eligible offer has a confirmed same-benefit counterpart in the existing feed. If only numerical identities match, say **threshold-qualifying rows, all apparent identity duplicates; benefit equivalence unverified**. Never subtract unresolved identities into a falsely precise eligible union.

## Resolving vendor conflicts before admission

There is no demonstrated rule in local primary sources making the outer ID or nested ID universally authoritative. Do not silently choose one, attach a nested store's title/code to another ID's price/stock, use fuzzy names, or derive a replacement from `document_id`/`sub_vendor_id` without a verified contract. [Independent adapter requirement](better-buy-expansion-spec.md#d03-keep-provider-shapes-independent), [prior conflict](better-buy-s05-feed-evidence.md#g02g04-observed-stock-and-price-fields-unresolved-identityterms).

A small read-only reconciliation can establish a dated row-level mapping using already observed, independently validated vendor codes and production-built paths:

1. Match both candidate IDs against the same-window baseline **product-level** vendor identities and codes; record exact code/title/ID agreement and all disagreements. Absence from Market Party is not invalidity, because that feed is not an exhaustive store directory.
2. Use a bounded store-home response at `/express-home/vendor/{code}` to identify which store the code represents, retaining explicit returned delivery facts separately. The spec documents this path as prior provider evidence, not a guarantee that every future response is an authority for all outer row fields.
3. Use the same campaign's bounded store-scoped concrete response at `/express-search/product-list/{id}/vendor/{code}`, when the endpoint contract is valid, to seek the exact product/variation, amount tuple and stock under an independently identified store. Exact agreement can corroborate ownership for that row. A product-only detail/minimum price or vendor preview cannot substitute for stock-backed confirmation.
4. Classify the conflict as resolved for the observed row only when the independently identified store and exact product/offer tuple agree. Preserve both raw IDs and provenance. If ambiguous, quarantine the row and keep the campaign gated pending a documented canonicalization policy and its tests. Never extrapolate one resolved row into a universal mapping for other stores/campaigns.

These routes are documented in the spec's [Snapp API use matrix](better-buy-expansion-spec.md#snapp-upstream-api-use-matrix). This audit itself issued no provider requests. The root investigation records whether these confirmation requests actually occurred and what they established.

## Applicability evidence

Positive stock and valid prices prove observed inventory and item-price arithmetic. They do not prove delivery to the saved location, benefit eligibility, validity period, quantity allowance, coupon/Pro/subsidy conditions, or a shared product-ID namespace. Empty badges, `collection_type=default`, campaign titles and absent terms are **unknown restrictions**, not affirmative universally applicable terms. Preserve explicit restrictions when provided. Compare Market Party and ordinary campaign offers only after their benefit identities are shown to coincide or are explicitly modeled separately. [D04–D05](better-buy-expansion-spec.md#d04-discount-and-stock-normalization), [G02–G04](better-buy-expansion-spec.md#mandatory-research-gates).

The independent account-plan gate does not invalidate read-only feed research. Estimated pages, observed request time, retry allowance and terminal evidence can prioritize a useful bounded candidate now; they remain local network measurements rather than production CPU/D1 capacity proof. This audit does not query the account, reinterpret a 403 response as a feed restriction, or assume a paid plan. [S05 scope](better-buy-expansion-spec.md#delivery-plan-for-to-tickets).
